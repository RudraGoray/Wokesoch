About

Woke Soch

A student-led social impact initiative built around three pillars: education, women's empowerment, and climate action. WS·MUN is run by the same team, not a separate events arm — the standards are the same ones the pillar work is held to.

WS·MUN

An MUN inside a social-impact organisation exists for one reason: to train the argument, not just stage it. Committee agendas here sit closer to the pillar work than a generic circuit topic list — disarmament, sustainable development, human rights, electoral reform, press accountability.

Frequently asked

What procedure does this conference run?

UNA-USA procedure. Points of order will be entertained. Personal attacks will not.

What's the dress code on camera?

Western business formal. The camera sees the top half. Your chair will assume the rest.

Are fees refundable?

Fees are non-refundable. Allocations are non-negotiable. Both are final.

What platform does the conference run on?

Zoom. Backgrounds may be virtual. Positions may not.
